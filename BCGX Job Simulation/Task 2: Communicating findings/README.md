## 🧠 Job Simulation: Communicating Insights

I’ve finished exploring the data — slicing it by **channel**, **campaign**, and **customer type** — and now it’s time to clearly and persuasively **communicate my insights**.  

💬 Different stakeholders have their own priorities and follow-up questions:  
- 📢 **Marketing** wants to know which message to scale.  
- 🌱 **Customer Growth** is focused on trends between *new* vs. *existing* customers.  

🎯 The client’s top priority is **growing new customer sales**, so my final recommendation needs to be **focused, relevant, and data-backed**.  

Here’s how I’m approaching it:  
- 📊 **Prioritize the right metrics.** I’m focusing on sales from *new customers*, not just total performance, to pinpoint where growth is really coming from.  
- 🔗 **Tie insights to the business question.** I’m not just naming the best-performing campaign — I’m explaining *why* it makes sense to double down on that campaign and channel combination based on the trends in the data.  
- 🧩 **Back up my claims with evidence.** I’ll use specific numbers — like sales figures, conversion rates, and consistent performance across channels — to support my recommendation.
