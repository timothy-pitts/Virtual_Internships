## Recommendation Email

To properly communicate my findings for the job simulation, I had to send a recommendation email to the sharholders of BCGX. I had to include the following:
- Briefly explain what data you reviewed (campaigns, channels, customer types)
- Highlight what the data showed—especially for new customers
- Recommend one campaign + channel combo to prioritize
- Use data to justify my recommendation
- Mention that my slide is attached and happy to discuss further

The following is the email I sent:

"Hello BCGX!

I reviewed over the raw data, channels, & campaign data. I also combined the channel & campaign data to compare & contrast campaign A & B's performance & discover which channel & method had the highest ROI.

Based on my findings, I recommend doubling down on Campaign A using emails.

Campaign A had the highest total conversion from users over the course of the week, with a total of 59 new customer conversions compared to Campaign B of only 34 new customers. This resulted in significantly higher sales of Campaign A at $3,726.99 compared to Campaign B's sales of $2,117.44.

Email proved to be the most effective method of converting users to customers, with Campaign A having $2,124.47 in sales which is more than double Instagram & web banners. Same is true for Campaign B with $1,382.38 for emails which is also more than double Instagram & web banners.

I've put together a slide that breaks down my findings further which is attached below and will happily discuss further & answer further questions. 

Sincerely,
Tim"
