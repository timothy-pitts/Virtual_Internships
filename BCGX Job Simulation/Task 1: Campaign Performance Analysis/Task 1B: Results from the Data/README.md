## 📊 Results From the Data [TimBCGX-VirtualInternship]

🧠 The following questions I began asking when digging into the data:  
- 🔍 What changes do we see when isolating **new customer performance**?  
- 📈 Are there different trends compared to **total sales**?  
- 💬 Why might a certain **message + channel** combo work best for new customers?  

📂 After adding a pivot table, inserting a slicer, and combing through the dataset, here’s what I discovered:  
- ✉️ **Email** had the highest overall sales, making it the strongest performer for revenue generation — a reliable pick when ROI is the main goal.  
- 💸 **Campaign B’s** more promotional message style resulted in higher total sales, proving broadly effective across audiences.  
- ⚙️ When combining top-performing elements, **Email + Campaign B** emerged as the highest-revenue pairing — strong channel + strong message.  
- 📱 After filtering by conversions, customers who engaged with **Instagram** spent the longest average time on site — a great opportunity for future retargeting!  
- 🌱 **Campaign A** (informal tone) + **Email** (personal channel) was most effective at converting **new customers** — a key insight for future growth and outreach.
