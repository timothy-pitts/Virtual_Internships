## Task 1: Campaign Performance Analysis
The first task of the job simulation is analyzing the most effective marketing campaign ads. 

For context, the partner company of BCGX, **NewCo**, wrapped up a one-week, multi-channel marketing campaign aimed at testing messaging and customer response across several featured retail products.  

🧩 The campaign included two message styles:  
- **Campaign A** — used an *informal, conversational tone*.  
- **Campaign B** — used a *sales-focused, promotional tone*.  

📣 The marketing efforts ran across three channels:  
- ✉️ **Email**  
- 📸 **Instagram**  
- 🖥️ **Website Banner**  

💡 The client’s main question for me:  
> “Which campaign + channel combination should we double down on to grow new customer sales—and why?”  

🧠 Before I dive into analyzing data, I’m thinking through a few key points:  
- Which *message tone* might connect better with first-time customers?  
- Which *channels* are most likely to reach brand-new audiences?
